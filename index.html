How it works

Collect without limits.

Books, coins, stamps, miniature cars, cameras, art, vintage technology, everyday objects, and categories nobody has named yet. Seekase keeps the structure flexible and the experience simple.

01 / ORGANIZE

Build your cabinets

Create collections, add photographs and useful details, and keep each object connected to its story.

02 / SHOW

Open a personal museum

Present your collection through an image-led public profile designed around the objects themselves.

03 / CONNECT

Discover collectors

Follow people, appreciate collections, save references, leave comments, and continue private collector conversations.

Sign in your way

One collector identity.

Begin with a compatible Solana wallet or email. Google and Apple are planned for a later release. When an additional method becomes available, it can be linked from account settings with fresh account verification.

01 / WALLET

Solana wallet

Priority entry on Solana Seeker, with private wallet verification and a public profile that never exposes the address.

02 / EMAIL

Email account

Use email and password to keep an account, catalog, social activity, and safety controls connected.

COMING LATER

Google and Apple

Planned sign-in options. They will remain clearly marked as unavailable until the providers and secure linking flow are live.

Daily check-in

A small ritual, honestly recorded.

Collectors with a connected wallet can check in once per UTC day. A successful check-in can build a 7, 14, or 30-day streak; it never mints a reward automatically or charges an application fee.

One per day

Approve a Memo

The wallet shows and approves one Solana Mainnet Memo transaction. The wallet pays only the disclosed network fee.

Your choice

Keep the proof private

The transaction is public on Solana, but raw wallet identity is not shown on a Seekase collector profile.

Inside Seekase

A cabinet you can carry.

Browse living collections, shape a public collector profile, and keep the details that make each object worth remembering.

Identity and trust

Proof without exposure.

Seekase is for collectors everywhere. Solana and Seeker support identity and participation where useful, while the collection and the collector remain at the center.

Seeker verified

Recognize genuine collectors

Eligible Seeker Genesis Token holders can receive a derived public badge after private Mainnet verification.

Wallet private

Keep the address out of the profile

Raw wallet addresses, token accounts, balances, and SGT mint details are not placed on public collector profiles.

Collector-first

Not a marketplace.

Seekase is for organizing, showing, and discussing the objects that matter to you. There is no checkout, escrow, payment flow, or investment product.

No transactions

Context before commerce

Collections are built around stories, provenance, photographs, and conversation—not listings or speculative value.

Global collectors

Designed beyond one device

Seekase welcomes every kind of collection. Seeker support is one entry point, not the definition of the community.

Roadmap

Growing with care.

The first release focuses on Android collectors. These later milestones are intentions, not promises or available features.
