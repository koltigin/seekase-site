Usage

Keep the mark intact.

The Seekase wordmark is custom artwork and should never be recreated with typed text.

Please do

Use approved files

Preserve proportions, use the correct contrast version, leave clear space, and keep the standalone mark at least 24 px wide.

Please avoid

Redrawing or implying endorsement

Do not stretch, rotate, outline, recolor individual parts, add effects, or suggest that asset use represents an official partnership.
