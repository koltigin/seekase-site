About Seekase

Every collection is a way of seeing.

Seekase is a mobile-first social home for collectors: a place to organize meaningful objects, preserve their context, and discover the people behind other personal museums.

The purpose

Keep more than a list.

A collection can hold memory, research, craft, culture, and identity. Seekase gives collectors a visual space for the details that ordinary feeds and marketplace listings leave behind.

01 — PRESERVE

Give objects context

Record photographs, makers, dates, editions, provenance, notes, and the personal story that makes an object worth keeping.

02 — EXPRESS

Shape a collector identity

Build public cabinets around interests and expertise without reducing a collection to a price or transaction.

03 — DISCOVER

Meet through collections

Find unfamiliar categories, thoughtful cabinets, and collectors who care about the same details.

Product principles

Objects first. People close behind.

Seekase is designed as a showcase and social network. Web3 supports identity and verifiable participation where useful; it does not define the entire experience.

01

Open-ended collecting

Books, coins, art, cameras, miniatures, archival material, everyday objects, and categories still waiting to be named all belong.

02

Not a marketplace

Seekase has no checkout, escrow, or speculative ranking. Conversation and context come before commerce.

03

Identity with boundaries

Your Seekase account, public collector profile, and private wallet identity remain separate by design.

04

Honest verification

Badges represent real, defined conditions. Raw wallet addresses, balances, token accounts, and private credentials do not appear on public profiles.

Independent by design

Built by KolTigin.

Seekase is independently developed and operated by KolTigin for collectors around the world. The project began with a simple idea: the things people choose to keep deserve a better digital home.
